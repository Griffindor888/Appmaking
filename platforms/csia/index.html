Cyber Security Insurance Australia · CSiA

Cyber-risk readiness and insurance pathways built on evidence.

CSiA is positioned to connect cyber controls, assurance and evidence with appropriately authorised insurance relationships. It is not represented as an insurer or underwriter unless and until that authority exists.

Readiness

Evidence before placement.

Cyber controls, supplier verification, awareness, incident readiness and other evidence can be structured before insurance engagement.

Assurance

Current state, not a static questionnaire.

WARDALE and Solurius can contribute evidence about authority, workforce assurance and operational controls.

Partner-dependent

Regulated activity remains with authorised parties.

Insurance advice, broking, underwriting or placement must remain within the authority of appropriately licensed or authorised partners.

Feedback

Risk requirements return to the ecosystem.

Insurance-readiness expectations can inform governance, security priorities and workforce assurance.
